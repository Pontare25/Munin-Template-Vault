## Construct By Dee
Link to his Youtube channel: [Construct By Dee - YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@construct_by_dee)

Dee (I think) is the originator of the logging system that is used in this system. The principle is pretty simple but get's exponentially powerful the more you write. Essentially he has "hijacked" the Dataview plugin's task functionality to query inline logs of information that allow for crosslinking and querying of files. I, personally, think this is the most powerful contribution anyone has made in the Obsidian community.

**Recommended videos and playlists**
- [The Daily Note Is The Only Note That Matters](https://youtu.be/Mt8mNIcoxYM?si=1pu2rlqBziLFi2lX)
- [I Hijacked The Task Feature](https://youtu.be/lvNRG2x1ejs?si=BDCS6m0hrCLC9HIB)
- [Here's My Obsidian Project Setup in 10 Minutes](https://youtu.be/oYrX0-b1-04?si=wJaEwOgTOIM3_9cx)
- [How to Manage Projects in Obsidian (Meetings, Tasks, Sub-Projects, Logs) - Guide 1](https://youtu.be/ytYnNmMlUcA?si=DRoVGhbxvFe0rEQN)
- [Start Here: Obsidian Bases (Video 1/4 of the Complete Series)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZ5QC7yloCc)
