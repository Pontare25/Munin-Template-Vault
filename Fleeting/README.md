# Fleeting

Quick capture for **your own notes and ideas** — a place to write something down without deciding where it belongs yet. New notes default here so capture stays frictionless; organizing later is the AI's job, not yours at the moment of thought.

`Fleeting/` is one half of the capture layer:

- **`Fleeting/`** — your own thoughts, ideas, and quick notes. Zero structure required; get it out of your head first.
- **`Raw/`** — source material you bring in (articles, PDFs, links, clippings) awaiting processing.

Both are in transit, not an archive. The AI develops fleeting notes and promotes the ones worth keeping into `Atlas/` (the permanent, compounding knowledge layer); sources in `Raw/` get processed the same way. Nothing here needs to be filed by hand.

## How capture works

New notes default to this folder, and a Templater **folder template** applies automatically to anything created here: you get `type: fleeting` and a timestamp for free, then just type. No template picking, no filing decision at the moment of thought. `Fleeting/` is the only auto-templated folder — everywhere else you choose a template when you promote the note.

When a capture is worth keeping, click the **Promote note** button at the top of it and pick the type. The note gets that type's frontmatter and sections, moves to that type's folder, and keeps everything you wrote. Outside `Fleeting/` the same thing runs from the command palette as *Promote note*. See [[Hotkeys]].

## What is waiting

Every Fleeting note that has not been promoted or deleted yet, oldest first, with how long it has been sitting here. The **Stale** view is the nagging half. Both capture lanes side by side live on the [[Inbox|Inbox dashboard]].
