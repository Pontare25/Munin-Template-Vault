# Fleeting

Quick capture for **your own notes and ideas** — a place to write something down without deciding where it belongs yet. New notes default here so capture stays frictionless; organizing later is the AI's job, not yours at the moment of thought.

`Fleeting/` is one half of the capture layer:

- **`Fleeting/`** — your own thoughts, ideas, and quick notes. Zero structure required; get it out of your head first.
- **`Raw/`** — source material you bring in (articles, PDFs, links, clippings) awaiting processing.

Both are in transit, not an archive. The AI develops fleeting notes and promotes the ones worth keeping into `Atlas/` (the permanent, compounding knowledge layer); sources in `Raw/` get processed the same way. Nothing here needs to be filed by hand.
